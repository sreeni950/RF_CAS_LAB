Research

Integrated circuits for intelligent physical systems

We develop circuit and system architectures that connect sensing, communication, and computation across wireless and flexible platforms.

01 · Wireless ICs

High-Speed Wireless Integrated Circuits

We develop RF, millimeter-wave, and mixed-signal integrated circuits for wideband, highly linear, and energy-efficient transceivers. Current directions include phased arrays, multibeam architectures, time-domain processing, current-mode signal processing, and reconfigurable radio front ends.

02 · Flexible Electronics

Flexible Antenna Arrays

We explore integrated circuits and antenna systems for flexible, conformal, wearable, and dynamically reconfigurable platforms.
