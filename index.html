Research Areas

Hardware for the next wireless frontier

Our work spans circuit, architecture, and system innovation with an emphasis on high performance, reconfigurability, and low-power operation.

01

High-Speed Wireless ICs

RF, mm-wave, and mixed-signal integrated circuits for wideband, highly linear, and energy-efficient wireless systems.

02

Flexible Array ICs

Integrated circuits and antenna-array platforms for conformal, flexible, and dynamically reconfigurable antenna arrays.
